Motivation

  • DESTINY provides analytical modeling for 2D/3D SRAM and eDRAM caches across 22–200nm.
  • Final goal: trace scaling trends across nodes and quantify sensitivity to TSV assumptions (projection, redundancy, hop model).
  • Scope is intentionally “input-driven” (no deep refactor), matching OH guidance: change inputs, observe trends, compare technologies.

Method

  • Baseline normalization: same cache capacity (2MB), word width (256b), associativity (1), roadmap (LOP), temperature (350K).
  • Scaling sweep: 65/45/32/22nm for SRAM (2D/3D) and eDRAM (2D/3D).
  • TSV sweep: SRAM 3D fine (4-layer) at 65nm, varying redundancy and hop model.
Configs: config/final/templates → generated & run via scripts/final_*.ps1.
